About DMU

Our history, mission and values.

Our Mission

Debre Markos Institute of Technology is dedicated to providing high-quality, accessible education in engineering, natural science, and technology disciplines, empowering graduates to drive economic and social development in Ethiopia.

Our Vision

To be a center of excellence in technological education and research, recognized at national and international levels for producing innovative, ethical, and competent graduates.
